## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze restaurant and food delivery data inspired by Swiggy. It focuses on restaurant distribution, revenue, food delivery orders, customer ratings, food types, and delivery time across different areas and cities.

The dashboard combines Excel-based data analysis, summary metrics, charts, and interactive filters to transform raw restaurant data into meaningful insights.

It provides a consolidated view of restaurant performance, food preferences, delivery efficiency, and area-wise comparisons through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze the total number of restaurants and delivery orders.
- Evaluate overall revenue and average restaurant ratings.
- Identify popular food types based on restaurant counts.
- Compare customer ratings across different food types.
- Analyze average delivery time and area-wise delivery performance.
- Compare restaurants based on delivery time.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for restaurant and food delivery analysis.
- Develop practical skills in Excel data analysis and visualization.

## ✨ Key Features

- Total restaurant KPI
- Total revenue KPI
- Average ratings KPI
- Average delivery time analysis
- Total delivery order analysis
- Top food types by restaurant count
- Food-type-wise rating comparison
- Area-wise delivery time analysis
- Restaurant-wise delivery time comparison
- Address-wise delivery time visualization
- City-based filtering using interactive selection controls
- KPI cards and charts for quick performance monitoring
- Consolidated dashboard for restaurant analytics

## 📊 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 🏪 Restaurant Analysis

Analyzed restaurant records to understand the number of restaurants represented in the dataset and compare restaurant activity across selected locations.

### 2. 💰 Revenue Analysis

Used the revenue summary KPI to provide an overview of the financial metric displayed on the dashboard.

### 3. ⭐ Rating Analysis

Compared average restaurant ratings and food-type-wise ratings to understand differences in customer feedback.

### 4. 🍕 Food Type Analysis

Analyzed food types by restaurant count to identify popular categories, including Indian, North Indian, Fast Food, Chinese, and other cuisines.

### 5. 🚚 Delivery Time Analysis

Examined average delivery time and compared delivery times across different areas and restaurant locations.

### 6. 📍 Area & Address Analysis

Used area and address-based comparisons to identify locations with different delivery-time patterns.

### 7. 📊 Dashboard & Data Visualization

Combined KPI cards, bar charts, a pie chart, a line chart, and city filters into a single dashboard for quick analysis and reporting.

## 🔄 From Raw Data to Business Insights

This project follows a structured workflow to transform restaurant data into useful business information.

| **Step** | **Process** | **Purpose** |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Raw Data | Explore restaurant and food delivery records |
| 02 | Data Preparation | Organize restaurant, price, rating, food type, and delivery-time fields |
| 03 | KPI Analysis | Summarize restaurant count, revenue, ratings, and delivery orders |
| 04 | Food Type Analysis | Compare food categories and their ratings |
| 05 | Delivery Analysis | Examine area-wise and restaurant-wise delivery times |
| 06 | Dashboard Creation | Present KPIs, charts, and interactive filters |
| 07 | Business Insights | Identify restaurant, cuisine, and delivery performance patterns |
